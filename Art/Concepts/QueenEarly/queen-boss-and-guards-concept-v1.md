## 정확한 생성 프롬프트

```text
Create a pixel-art character design concept sheet for Grid-Caster, a top-down 2D tile-based tactical game. Show a large crowned queen monster boss together with her smaller royal guards: an armored cavalry knight, a fire-wielding armadillo bishop, and a castle-wall-mounted crossbow rook. Design the queen for an approximately 120-by-120-pixel sprite footprint, presented enlarged so the pixel shapes are visible. Her design should express her straight and diagonal attacks, localized area bombardment, and temporary wall creation. Present the queen prominently and show the three guard designs separately at a visibly smaller game scale. Match the existing game's pixel-art style and viewing angle using the reference images. This is a character design sheet for review.

Input images:
- armadillo_sheet.png: existing monster pixel-art style reference.
- new_wizard-Sheet.png: existing player pixel-art style and viewing-angle reference.
```
